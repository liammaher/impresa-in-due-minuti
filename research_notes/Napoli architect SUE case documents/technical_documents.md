## 1. Relazione tecnica asseverata / relazione tecnico-illustrativa: sections, facts stated, signer

### Takeaway
There are two distinct things: (a) the **relazione tecnica di asseverazione** of the modulo unificato, a tick-box declaration in ~22 numbered quadri (intervento category, dati geometrici, zona urbanistica, barriere, impianti, energia, acustica, risulta, incendi, amianto, igienico-sanitaria, sismica, then one quadro per vincolo), and (b) the **free-text relazione tecnico-descrittiva** the architect writes and attaches. In Napoli the CILA model folds the asseverazione into "CILA.a" and the notes require a free-text relazione stating PRG discipline, conformity and legittimità (art. 27 and 21 RE). Signer: the progettista, who assumes the role of "persona esercente un servizio di pubblica necessità" (artt. 359/481 c.p.); in Napoli every file is singly digitally signed by the progettista.

### Inferences
- For a typical Napoli apartment CILA (manutenzione straordinaria leggera, interior walls, bathroom, impianti), the facts a form asks that sit in the relazione are: art. 3 category + Tabella A activity number, PRG zone and article (Variante generale: zona A centro storico vs B etc., with the tipo edilizio in zona A), no art. 23-ter change of use, L.13/89 status (usually "non soggetto" or adattabilità for a residential unit), impianti letters under DM 37/2008, energy "non soggetto" unless envelope/heating works, DPCM 5/12/1997 status, structural irrelevance (quadro 12.3), and each vincolo yes/no.

## 2. Elaborati grafici, documentazione fotografica, stato legittimo documentation

### Takeaway
**[NA]** Napoli requires drawings in a single ISO 216 sheet set (A4/A3/A2), allowing immediate comparison of stato di fatto (legittimo) vs progetto: inquadramento ≥ 1:5000, PRG extract, plans/elevations/sections/details ≥ 1:100 with materials, quote, superfici and destinazione d'uso of every room; external spaces 1:200 and 1:500; plus colour photos with coni ottici. The stato legittimo rests on the titles the owner supplies (with number and date); the national SCIA form asks for those titles directly.

## 3. What the modulo unificato CILA/SCIA asks directly vs only as attachments; tolleranze (art. 34-bis)

### Takeaway
The titolare's modulo asks directly: titolarità, parti comuni/condominio, other communications attached, intervention qualification and dates, catasto, contributo (gratuito/oneroso and how paid), impresa esecutrice (via soggetti coinvolti), and the D.Lgs. 81/2008 block (cantiere size ≥/< 200 uomini-giorno, notifica). The progettista's part asks (SCIA) all the relazione quadri and (CILA and SCIA, post-Salva Casa) a tolleranze block by unit surface band and seismic zone. Everything else (drawings, photos, DM 37 project, Legge 10 relazione, sismica filings, paesaggistica, DURC) is an attachment.

### Cited Findings

## 4. Impresa esecutrice, DURC, organico medio annuo, notifica preliminare (art. 99 D.Lgs. 81/2008)

### Takeaway
The committente gives the Comune, before works start, the impresa's identity, DURC (now acquired d'ufficio by the PA for private works) and the declaration of organico medio annuo and CCNL (art. 90 c.9). The notifica preliminare (Allegato XII) goes to ASL and Ispettorato (and cassa edile) before works when there are several firms (art. 90 c.3), when variations bring the site into that case, or for a single firm with ≥ 200 uomini-giorno. **[NA]** Napoli lists notifica and DURC as pre-start documentation and DURC again at fine lavori; missing art. 90/99 obligations suspend the title.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 99 c.1: committente or responsabile dei lavori sends, before works, to ASL and "direzione provinciale del lavoro" (prefetto only for public works) the notifica per Allegato XII, in (a) cantieri of art. 90 c.3 (more than one impresa), (b) cantieri that fall into (a) through variations, (c) cantieri with a single impresa whose presumed work is not below 200 uomini-giorno; c.1.1 (L. 215/2021) adds the cassa edile; c.1-bis creates an INL database fed by notifiche — [TUSSL art. 99](https://www.tussl.it/titolo-iv-cantieri-temporanei-o-mobili/capo-i-misure-per-la-salute-e-sicurezza-nei-cantieri-temporanei-o-mobili/art-99)
- Allegato XII content includes the date of the communication, site address, committente's data (name, surname, codice fiscale, address), nature of the work, and presumed total amount of works in euro (among others) — [Regione Piemonte DGR (via search summary)](https://www.regione.piemonte.it/governo/bollettino/abbonati/2024/08/attach/dgr_08197_1050_19022024.pdf)
- Model notifica preliminare (DOC), updated Nov 2025 to D.Lgs. 159/2025 — [Certifico, modello notifica preliminare](https://www.certifico.com/sicurezza-lavoro/documenti-sicurezza/modelli-sicurezza/modello-notifica-preliminare-cantieri); ACCA guide — [BibLus, notifica preliminare ASL](https://biblus.acca.it/notifica-preliminare-asl/)
- **[NA]** Campania: Ordine Ingegneri Benevento hosts a regional decree on notifiche preliminari — [benevento.ordingegneri.it, DecretoNotifichePreliminari.pdf](https://benevento.ordingegneri.it/wp-content/uploads/sites/74/2023/01/DecretoNotifichePreliminari.pdf) (not read; title only)
- Art. 90 c.9: (a) committente checks idoneità tecnico-professionale, including DURC; (b) impresa's declaration of organico medio annuo by qualifica and of the CCNL applied; (c) the DURC obligation extends to firms using own employees without appalto; c.10: absence of DURC suspends the titolo abilitativo; Interpello 1/2016: for private works the PA acquires DURC d'ufficio — [ANCE, art. 90 commi 9 e 10](https://ance.it/2016/04/art-90-commi-9-e-10-del-d-lgs-n-81-2008-e-smi/?print=pdf); [PuntoSicuro, interpello](https://www.puntosicuro.it/edilizia-C-10/interpello-cosa-accade-in-assenza-del-durc-AR-15833/)
- **[NA]** Napoli pre-start documentation "se prevista": deroga acustica for cantieri (art. 12 piano di zonizzazione acustica, CC delibera n. 204 of 21/12/2001); "notifica preliminare di cui all'articolo 99 del Dlgs n.81/08"; DURC "rilasciato dall'INPS, dall'INAIL o dalle Casse edili all'inizio dei lavori" and the start communications to the enti and cassa edile; Legge 10 deposit. Efficacy of the title "è sospesa" if artt. 90, 91, 99 and 100 obligations are not met. Site board (tabella di cantiere) with type of work, CILA protocol, committente, progettista, DL, CSE and, for DM 37 impianti, installer and impianti designer — [Napoli CILA notes](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a small apartment renovation with one firm under 200 uomini-giorno, no notifica is due (schema choice `inf200ggUomo` / `no_notifica`); if electricians/plumbers are separate firms, art. 90 c.3 applies (CSP/CSE appointment and notifica).
- The form-relevant facts: impresa ragione sociale, CF/P.IVA, sede, INPS/INAIL/Cassa edile positions (for DURC), organico medio annuo, CCNL; cantiere: uomini-giorno estimate, number of firms, dates, importo presunto.

### Gaps
- The full numbered Allegato XII list was not fetched (TUSSL page links it but the text was not read).
- Changes from D.Lgs. 159/2025 (GU 31.10.2025) to art. 99 not verified. The patente a crediti (art. 27, DL 19/2024) is believed to require the patente number for firms in cantieri; not verified here.
