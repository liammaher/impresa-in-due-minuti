## Walkthrough: case (a) CILA for an apartment renovation, case (b) the SCIA / zona A / vincolo variant

### Takeaway
Case (a), moving tramezzi, a new bathroom and new impianti with no structural work, is a CILA under art. 6-bis DPR 380 (manutenzione straordinaria, art. 3 c.1 lett. b). Since 6 July 2026 it is filed only on impresainungiorno, every file signed digitally. It costs €151 in Napoli diritti, paid before filing, and it needs a Napoli tax-regularity declaration (Programma 100), the regional Modello DP and, in zona A, an attestation of the building's "tipo edilizio". Case (b) becomes a SCIA once structural parts are touched (art. 22), adding the Genio Civile di Napoli seismic step. Façade work under a vincolo needs an autorizzazione paesaggistica from the Comune (Area Ambiente), a separate prior procedure, before the CILA/SCIA is sent.

### Cited Findings

**Step 0: which title (the architect's judgment).**
- **[NAZIONALE]** A CILA covers manutenzione straordinaria under art. 3 c.1 lett. b, "ivi compresa l'apertura di porte interne o lo spostamento di pareti interne, sempre che non riguardino le parti strutturali". It also covers restauro and risanamento conservativo not touching structure, and residual works not listed in artt. 6, 10 or 22 (Tabella A, d.lgs. 222/2016, attività 3, 5, 30). The Napoli CILA notes restate this. — [Napoli CILA note illustrative 23/06/2025, §1](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)
- **[NAPOLI]** To classify the works, the architect is told to use art. 3 c.1 and art. 6-bis c.1 DPR 380, plus **art. 10 of the NTA of the Variante generale al PRG** and Tabella A of d.lgs. 222/2016. The note adds: "le definizioni di cui ai citati articoli 3 e 6bis prevalgono sulle disposizioni degli strumenti urbanistici generali e dei regolamenti edilizi". — [CILA note, §5 d)](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)
- **[NAPOLI]** Who may file is checked against **art. 22 of the Regolamento Edilizio (RE)**. The asseverazione's technical report follows **art. 27 RE**, and the declaration that the existing works are legitimate follows **art. 21 RE**. — [CILA note, §5](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)
- **[NAZIONALE]** The forms adopted in Napoli list, among the attachments, a "Dichiarazione di assenso dei terzi titolari di altri diritti". This is how a tenant (or one co-owner) files with the owner's consent. — [DD 603/2025 with the CU 27/03/2025 forms (CILA/SCIA attachments table)](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/0603_2025.pdf)
- **[NAZIONALE, applied in Napoli]** In zona A, a change of use within the same functional category counts as heavy ristrutturazione. A practitioner blog reports the Cassazione confirmed this in 2026. **[FORUM/BLOG]** — [Studio Tecnico Pagliai, tag centro storico](https://www.studiotecnicopagliai.it/tag/centro-storico/)

**Step 1: survey and stato legittimo (see the next section for detail).**
- **[NAPOLI]** Under the "Regolarità urbanistica e precedenti edilizi" point of the Modello MDA, the regularity of what exists is shown "elencando gli estremi dei titoli abilitativi rilasciati riferiti al fabbricato quali Licenze/Concessioni/Permessi, autorizzazioni, DIA, SCIA, CILA che hanno interessato l'edificio". — [Linee Guida DD 501/2026, §7](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/LINEE-GUIDA_30.06.2026.pdf)

**Step 2: drawings and documents the architect produces.**
- **[NAPOLI]** The drawings show the stato di fatto (legittimo) and the progetto: a location plan at no less than 1:5000, an extract of the PRG, and plans, elevations and sections at no less than 1:100, with materials, levels, areas and the use of every room. They also include a technical report, colour photos with viewpoints (coni ottici), and either the L. 13/1989 and DM 236/89 accessibility check or a declaration of exemption. The report must state that the urbanistically relevant use category does not change (art. 23-ter). — [CILA note, documentation list](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)
- **[NAPOLI, new since 2026]** For work on existing buildings a **"Stato Comparativo" sheet is now mandatory**, coloured **yellow for demolitions, red for new construction** (green and blue for works abandoned during construction). There is a black-and-white stato attuale and stato di progetto with dimensions, a North-South arrow, and a landscape layout suited to 16:9 screens. — [Linee Guida DD 501/2026, §§5, 8, 9](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/LINEE-GUIDA_30.06.2026.pdf)
- **[NAPOLI]** File rules: native digital PDF/A (no scanned drawings, except special cases such as historic details), **20 MB per file**, at most 200 DPI. File names have no spaces, accents or special characters ("titolo_di_proprieta", not "titolo_di_proprietà"); the system rejects other names. **Every document and attachment is signed digitally.** Photos are a separate PDF/A, not inside a drawing sheet. For works inside the apartment only, photos show the affected parts with a wide view of the context. — [Linee Guida DD 501/2026, §§3, 4, 6](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/LINEE-GUIDA_30.06.2026.pdf)
- **[NAPOLI]** On impresainungiorno the architect also attaches a separate technical report, "a completamento della documentazione prodotta automaticamente dal sistema". It follows the PdC note illustrative. — [Linee Guida DD 501/2026, §2](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/LINEE-GUIDA_30.06.2026.pdf)
- **[NAPOLI]** Always-mandatory attachments in the forms Napoli adopted: the receipt for the diritti di segreteria; **"Modello DP – dichiarazione pagamento ai sensi della Legge regionale 59/2018"** (the professional declares the client has paid their fees) **[CAMPANIA]**; and **"Allegato A Nuovo Programma 100"**, a sworn declaration (art. 47 DPR 445/2000) about the applicant's position on Comune di Napoli taxes and dues **[NAPOLI]**. An ID copy is needed only when the parties don't sign digitally and/or there is no procura/delega. — [DD 603/2025 forms, attachments table](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/0603_2025.pdf); [CILA note](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf); Programma 100 form: https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Allegato_A_nuovo_p100.pdf; Modello DP: https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/MOD.DP_Dichiarazione_di_Pagamento.pdf
- **[NAZIONALE]** The impianti project follows art. 5 DM 37/08 (schemes, plans, technical report). The energy report follows d.lgs. 192/05, art. 8, filed before work starts (Napoli schema RE, allegato MD.3). — [CILA note](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)
- **[NAPOLI]** If the apartment is split into two units (frazionamento under art. 22 c.2 of the Variante generale al PRG, as approved by DCC n. 24 of 20/04/2017), a transcribed **atto d'obbligo** that the unit will not be split further is required, sent with the fine lavori (schema Fcs in MD.3). — [CILA note, §5 d.4](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)

**Step 3: zona A (centro storico).**
- **[NAPOLI]** "Nel caso di interventi in centro storico (zona A) della Variante generale al prg occorre allegare idonea documentazione, sottoscritta ai sensi e per gli effetti dell'articolo 481 del codice penale da professionista abilitato, che attesti la corrispondenza dell'unità edilizia oggetto di intervento al relativo tipo edilizio codificato nelle Norme di attuazione… (articolo 63, comma 4 della Variante generale al prg)". — [CILA note, documentation](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)
- **[NAPOLI]** For buildings of particular historic or architectural value, the photos must show any functional, typological, hygienic or static decay, and the typological, formal and structural elements that qualify the building. — [Linee Guida DD 501/2026, §6 c)](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/LINEE-GUIDA_30.06.2026.pdf)

**Step 4: atti di assenso (paesaggio, Soprintendenza, other).**
- **[NAPOLI]** Where an autorizzazione paesaggistica (art. 146 d.lgs. 42/04) is needed, "questa va acquisita come procedimento autonomo e presupposto prima dell'inoltro della Cila". — [servizionline.comune.napoli.it, Edilizia Privata page](https://servizionline.comune.napoli.it/en/web/napoli/edilizia-privata)
- **[NAPOLI]** Other atti di assenso are looked up in Allegato AS.2 (a summary table, 2015). The need for them must be flagged to the SUE (sportello.unico.edilizia@comune.napoli.it), and "i lavori non potranno iniziare prima dell'acquisizione degli presupposti". Assents already obtained are attached. — [CILA note, §3 atti di assenso](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf); AS.2: https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/AS_2_prospetto_sintesi_atti_assenso_20_8_15.pdf (its text layer is font-encoded, so I could not read it)
- **[NAPOLI]** CILAs that break the PRG or sector rules (seismic, fire, hygiene, energy, d.lgs. 42/04), or that lack the essential documents, are **inefficaci**. — [CILA note, §4](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)

**Step 7: after filing: controls, start and end of works.**
- **[NAPOLI]** CILAs are checked by random sample (determina dirigenziale 128 of 17/01/2017); the lists drawn are published. — [CILA note, §3](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf); [servizionline page](https://servizionline.comune.napoli.it/en/web/napoli/edilizia-privata); [DD 913/2024, art. 4 c.2](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/disposizione_dirigenziale_913_2024_approvazione_circolare_adempimenti_sanatoaria_edilizia.pdf)
- **[NAZIONALE]** The commune has no peremptory deadline for checking a CILA (unlike the 30 days for a SCIA), only repressive and sanctioning powers. **[secondary]** — [ediltecnico, "Controlli sulla CILA"](https://ediltecnico.it/controlli-cila/); [lavoripubblici.it, "CILA inefficace"](https://www.lavoripubblici.it/news/cila-inefficace-quando-non-vale-poteri-comune-37908)
- **[NAPOLI]** Before work starts, where applicable:
  - acoustic waiver for the building site (piano di zonizzazione acustica, DCC 204/2001, art. 12)
  - notifica preliminare (art. 99 d.lgs. 81/08)
  - DURC
  - deposit of the energy report
  - a site sign naming the CILA's protocol number and the people involved

  At the end: COM form for fine lavori with the direttore lavori's asseverazione, DURC, DM 37/08 conformity, catasto update within 30 days (or a declaration that none is needed), proof of waste disposal, APE where due, and acoustic test where due. — [CILA note, "Adempimenti…"](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)

**Case (b): SCIA, structure and seismic.**
- **[CAMPANIA]** Seismic rules are L.R. 9/1983 and Regolamento regionale 4/2010. "Interventi rilevanti" need a seismic **autorizzazione**; "lavori minori", which include repairs and local interventions on existing buildings, follow a simplified deposit. Filing is on the regional telematic platform, and the competent office is the Genio Civile di Napoli. **[secondary]** — [ingenio-web, autorizzazione sismica o denuncia](https://www.ingenio-web.it/articoli/autorizzazione-sismica-o-denuncia-dei-lavori-genio-civile-o-comune-le-regole-per-le-costruzioni-in-zona-sismica/); [Reg. 4/2010 coordinato (Ordine Ingegneri Benevento)](https://benevento.ordingegneri.it/wp-content/uploads/sites/74/2022/03/REGOLAM_4_2010_coordinato.pdf)
- **[NAPOLI]** Napoli's sanatoria circular names the structural rules every filing must meet: "capi II e IV del Dpr n.380/2001 smi, Legge regionale n.9/1983 smi e Regolamento della Regione Campania n.4/2010 smi". It also names the idrogeologico rule, art. 24 of the Variante generale al PRG and the PSAI. — [DD 913/2024, art. 2 c.2](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/disposizione_dirigenziale_913_2024_approvazione_circolare_adempimenti_sanatoaria_edilizia.pdf)
- **[NAZIONALE]** The forms Napoli adopted have a section "15) Interventi strutturali e/o in zona sismica", and a tolerances section that asks for the seismic zone (zones 1-2 vs 3-4) and any autorizzazione sismica. — [DD 603/2025 forms](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/0603_2025.pdf)

**Case (b): landscape in Napoli.**

The Commissione locale per il paesaggio gives its opinion, then the Soprintendenza ABAP per il Comune di Napoli gives the binding one, sometimes with prescriptions, e.g. that the inizio lavori also go to the Soprintendenza. — MIT conference-of-services files: [Prot 16156 28/08/2024 Parere rilascio PAES Comune NA](https://mit.gov.it/nfsmitgov/files/media/conferenza-servizio/2025-01/Prot_16156_28082024_Parere_rilascio_PAES_Comune_NA.pdf); [Prot 17248 17/09/2024 Parere SABAP-NA](https://mit.gov.it/nfsmitgov/files/media/conferenza-servizio/2025-01/Prot_17248_17092024_Parere_SABAP-NA.pdf); [Prot 5790 21/03/2025 Aut Paes](https://mit.gov.it/nfsmitgov/files/media/conferenza-servizio/2025-03/5_Prot_5790_21032025_TX_Aut_Paes.pdf)

These are from the statute, which I did not re-fetch in this session. — [DPR 31/2017 on Normattiva](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:decreto.del.presidente.della.repubblica:2017-02-13;31); [DPR 31/2017 text (Regione Emilia-Romagna copy)](https://territorio.regione.emilia-romagna.it/codice-territorio/paesaggio/DPR_31_2017_autorizzazione_paesaggistica.pdf/@@download/file/DPR_31_2017_autorizzazione_paesaggistica.pdf)

**Sanctions: the CILA tardiva.**
- **[NAZIONALE, Napoli amounts]** Works already done without a CILA: **€1,000 oblazione** (MCILA). A CILA filed spontaneously while works are under way: **€333**. — [CILA note](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf); [DD 913/2024, art. 4 c.3](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/disposizione_dirigenziale_913_2024_approvazione_circolare_adempimenti_sanatoaria_edilizia.pdf). An MCILA without proof of the diritti and the €1,000 sanction is "priva di effetti e dichiarata irricevibile" (same DD 913, art. 4 c.3).
- **[NAPOLI, Dec 2024 / Jun 2025]** The MCILA, SCIA in sanatoria (artt. 34-ter c.3, 36-bis c.1) and PdC in sanatoria (artt. 36, 36-bis) were to be filed **on paper at the Protocollo Generale, Palazzo San Giacomo**, plus a CD or USB stick when assents are needed, "fino a nuove disposizioni" and until the SISDOC application was updated. — [DD 913/2024, art. 2](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/disposizione_dirigenziale_913_2024_approvazione_circolare_adempimenti_sanatoaria_edilizia.pdf); [DD 603/2025, dispositivo §3](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/0603_2025.pdf); [CILA note, d.3](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf). These conflict with DD 501/2026's "esclusivamente" and its ban on paper (see Gaps).
- **[NAPOLI]** Sanatoria oblazioni under art. 36-bis: double the contributo di costruzione (+20% for partial non-conformity or essential variations, except double conformity); or double the increase in market value as assessed by the Agenzia delle Entrate, between €1,032 and €10,328 (€516 to €5,164 if double-conforming). Napoli's formula is OB = 2×AVV×[(Vmax−Vmin)/100,000]+Vmin. Choosing the maximum avoids the Agenzia's valuation, whose fee (€379 per person-day) the applicant pays. — [DD 913/2024, artt. 6, 8](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/disposizione_dirigenziale_913_2024_approvazione_circolare_adempimenti_sanatoaria_edilizia.pdf)

## Stato legittimo (art. 9-bis DPR 380/2001 after Salva Casa) in practice in Napoli

### Takeaway
Napoli makes the architect list every title that touched the building (licenze, concessioni, permessi, DIA, SCIA, CILA), plus condoni and sanatorie, and assert that the current state matches. The national post-Salva Casa forms let pre-1967 or no-title cases rest on catasto di primo impianto or other evidence (photos, maps, archive records), and record tolerances under artt. 34-bis and 34-ter. A CILA on a property whose stato legittimo can't be shown is exposed to inefficacia.

### Cited Findings
- **[NAPOLI]** "la regolarità delle preesistenze deve essere dimostrata elencando gli estremi dei titoli abilitativi rilasciati riferiti al fabbricato…". For demolition and reconstruction, a sworn survey with drawings and photos is required. — [Linee Guida DD 501/2026, §7](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/LINEE-GUIDA_30.06.2026.pdf)
- **[NAZIONALE, post-Salva Casa]** The forms Napoli adopted offer:
  - the list of titles (g.1.x, including SCIA in sanatoria art. 36-bis);
  - "g.2 immobile realizzato in un'epoca in cui non era obbligatorio un titolo abilitativo" and "g.3 non sono disponibili la copia o gli estremi del titolo… ma sussiste un principio di prova documentale", both supported by "copia accatastamento di primo impianto" or "altri documenti probanti (riprese fotografiche, estratti cartografici, documenti di archivio…) a norma dell'art. 9-bis, comma 1-bis, quarto e quinto periodo";
  - g.4, sanctions paid (artt. 33, 34, 37, 38);
  - g.5, tolerance declarations already filed (artt. 34-bis, 34-ter c.4);
  - g.6, full conformity or conformity with the tolerances declared.

  — [DD 603/2025 forms](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/0603_2025.pdf)
- **[NAZIONALE]** The forms' tolerance section asks whether the building is in seismic zone 1-2 or 3-4. In zones 1-2 the technician attests whether the tolerance matters structurally, and may attach the autorizzazione sismica. For buildings under d.lgs. 42/2004, art. 2 c.1 DPR 31/2017 does not apply to some tolerances. — [DD 603/2025 forms, tolerances section](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/0603_2025.pdf)
- **[NAPOLI]** Sanatoria acts take effect only once paid: "in mancanza gli stessi sono privi di efficacia e non concorrono alla determinazione dello stato legittimo". — [DD 913/2024, art. 3 c.2](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/disposizione_dirigenziale_913_2024_approvazione_circolare_adempimenti_sanatoaria_edilizia.pdf)
- **[NAPOLI]** If documents are already held by the Comune, the applicant gives the references needed to find them; self-declarations under DPR 445/2000 are allowed, with random checks. — [CILA note, §3](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)
- **[NAZIONALE, case law]**
  - A June 2026 TAR Sicilia ruling held a CILA ineffective when stato legittimo can't be shown; a CILA is not a sanatoria by the back door. — [lavoripubblici.it, TAR Sicilia 1427/2026](https://www.lavoripubblici.it/news/cila-stato-legittimo-immobile-senza-titolo-tar-sicilia-1427-2026-38292)
  - The Consiglio di Stato holds that the CILAS's simplified stato-legittimo regime does not extend to the whole building. **[blog]** — [Studio Pagliai 2026](https://www.studiotecnicopagliai.it/cila-immobili-irregolari-poteri-repressivi-sanzionatori-comunali-2026/)
  - Where a file can't be found, the PA must certify in writing that it does not exist. **[blog]** — [Studio Pagliai](https://www.studiotecnicopagliai.it/stato-legittimo-immobile-comune-deve-attestare-insussistenza-pratica-edilizia/)
- **[NAPOLI, case law]** TAR Campania (Napoli) cases on access to building files: permits are subject to maximum transparency, and the Comune's silence on an access request was held unlawful. — [TAR Campania Napoli 3897/2023 (PDF)](https://www.donnegeometra.it/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/tar-campania-napoli-3897-2023-accesso-civico-permesso-di-costruire.pdf); [ediltecnico summary](https://ediltecnico.it/accesso-civico-permesso-costruire/)

### Inferences
- For a typical Napoli condominium: pre-1942 buildings in the centro storico predate any title requirement inside the centro abitato, so the national "epoch" option backed by catasto di primo impianto (Napoli's 1939-40 impianto) is the usual route. Typical problems: the plan in the licenza doesn't match the apartment (old tramezzi moves), condoni still open, the catasto plan differing from the title. These are inferences from the form structure and general practice, not from a Napoli source.
- The 34-bis tolerances (2-6% by unit size after Salva Casa) are what makes many old internal changes declarable without a sanatoria; the form's tolerance section is where this happens.

## Centro storico, zona A of the Variante generale al PRG, and the UNESCO site: extra rules for apartment works

### Takeaway
In zona A (the Variante generale for the centro storico, eastern and north-western zones, approved by DPGRC 323 of 11 June 2004), every CILA or SCIA must carry a sworn attestation (art. 481 c.p.) that the building matches its "tipo edilizio" coded in the NTA (art. 63 c.4). The NTA's typological rules then limit what can change. UNESCO inscription alone adds no permit. A landscape or cultural-heritage permit comes only from a specific vincolo: art. 136 for the area, or art. 10/13 d.lgs. 42/2004 for the building. Façade work under a vincolo means an autorizzazione paesaggistica (DPR 31/2017) and/or the Soprintendenza.

### Cited Findings
- **[NAPOLI]** The art. 63 c.4 attestation of the tipo edilizio is required for any intervention in zona A. — [CILA note](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)
- **[NAPOLI]** The Variante generale for the centro storico, eastern and north-western zones was approved by DPGRC n. 323 of 11/06/2004 (BURC n. 29). — [astegiudiziarie.it, perizia NA-EI-328-2025](https://www.astegiudiziarie.it/allegato/AltroAllegato-NA-EI-328-2025-10.pdf/2320045). Zona A, "insediamenti di interesse storico", is governed by art. 26 of its NTA. — [MIT, Prot 16436 03/09/2024 Parere pianificazione urbanistica Comune NA](https://mit.gov.it/nfsmitgov/files/media/conferenza-servizio/2025-01/Prot_16436_03092024_Parere_Pianificazoine_urb_Comune_NA.pdf)
- **[NAPOLI]** The NTA's art. 10 helps define the intervention, and art. 22 c.2 (as approved by DCC 24/2017) governs frazionamenti. — [CILA note](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)
- **[NAZIONALE]** UNESCO status alone does not trigger Parte III of the Codice. A centro storico needs a specific declaration under art. 136 c.1 lett. c; where one exists, works need both the building title and the art. 146 authorisation. — [ANCE, "Centri storici: il punto sulla tutela"](https://ance.it/2014/04/centri-storici-il-punto-sulla-tutela/?print=pdf) (2014, pre-Salva Casa, but on d.lgs. 42/04, which Salva Casa didn't change)
- **[NAPOLI, case law via blog]** A TAR Campania case on a listed apartment in a historic palazzo in central Napoli: the Soprintendenza refused façade changes and new openings. The TAR said its opinion covers compatibility with heritage values, separate from building regularity, and must be clearly reasoned. **[secondary]** — [edilizia.com, "Ristrutturare un immobile vincolato"](https://www.edilizia.com/edilizia-urbanistica/ristrutturare-un-immobile-vincolato-quali-permessi-servono-davvero/)
- **[NAPOLI]** Photo rules for buildings of historic value. — [Linee Guida DD 501/2026, §6 c)](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/LINEE-GUIDA_30.06.2026.pdf)
- **[NAZIONALE]** DPR 31/2017 regime (Allegato A exemptions, Allegato B semplificata). — [DPR 31/2017 on Normattiva](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:decreto.del.presidente.della.repubblica:2017-02-13;31)
- **[NAPOLI, not about apartments]** A three-year ban on new restaurants, bars and pizzerie in the UNESCO centro storico, agreed with the Regione and the Soprintendenza; it concerns commerce only. — [Dissapore](https://www.dissapore.com/notizie/napoli-per-tre-anni-niente-nuovi-ristoranti-pizzerie-e-bar-nel-centro-storico/)

### Inferences
- For case (a) inside a zona A palazzo with no individual listing: a CILA plus the art. 63 c.4 attestation is enough for interior work. DPR 31/2017 A.1 exempts interior works from the paesaggistica even inside a vincolo area.
- If the palazzo is listed under art. 10 or 13 d.lgs. 42/04, the Soprintendenza's authorisation under art. 21 is needed even for interior work. That is a separate procedure, flagged to the SUE as an assent.
- For façade, window or balcony work on a building in a vincolo area: B.2 or B.3 semplificata through the Comune's Area Ambiente and the Soprintendenza, before the CILA or SCIA.
