# Public and professional lookups for a Napoli property (CILA/SCIA at the SUE)

Research date: 2026-10-31. "Verified" means I fetched the URL (WebFetch or curl) on that date and saw the content described. "Secondary" means a trade press or guide page reported it. "Inferred" means my reading, not shown by a source. curl probes ran from this Mac without authentication.

## For each lookup: exact URL, what you get, access barrier, automation feasibility, terms

### Takeaway
Four sources can be queried by machine without a login, and only these four: the cadastral map (AdE WMS/WFS, open data under CC-BY), the PRG plates and norms (static PDFs/ZIPs on comune.napoli.it), the Comune's stradario (CSV on its CKAN, with an API), and the national landscape-constraint maps (SITAP, an interactive page). Everything about the specific unit needs a login or a request: owner's visura and planimetria (SPID or SISTER), Vincoli in Rete (login page), the CDU (a request to the Comune), and the building-title archive (accesso agli atti to the SUE). The DURC check needs SPID, and the visura camerale costs a few euros.

### Cited Findings

#### 1. Cadastre (Agenzia delle Entrate)
- **Free cadastral map WMS (verified).** `https://wms.cartografia.agenziaentrate.gov.it/inspire/wms/ows01.php`. GetCapabilities returned 200 with layers `CP.CadastralParcel`, `CP.CadastralZoning`, `fabbricati`, `codice_plla` (parcel number labels), `strade`, `acque`, `vestizioni`. CRS include EPSG:6706 (RDN2008), 4258 and 25833. AccessConstraints read "Dato pubblico". No key or login — [WMS GetCapabilities](https://wms.cartografia.agenziaentrate.gov.it/inspire/wms/ows01.php?SERVICE=WMS&REQUEST=GetCapabilities)
- **Limit (verified by test):** a GetFeatureInfo on `CP.CadastralParcel` at three points in central Napoli (e.g. lon 14.2497, lat 40.8393) found a feature. In GML it returned only the parcel's bounding box, with no foglio/particella attributes. text/plain listed "Feature 1:" with no fields. Over WMS, then, the parcel number is drawn on the image (`codice_plla`) and is not returned as data. A point on a street returned "no results", since roads are not parcels — [same WMS endpoint, GetFeatureInfo]
- **WFS / bulk vector open data (secondary, endpoint partly verified).** Since early 2025 AdE has published a WFS for point queries plus bulk GML downloads of parcels, CC-BY 4.0, national coverage except Trento/Bolzano. One source says the bulk set refreshes every six months — [ediltecnico](https://ediltecnico.it/catasto-e-dati-territoriali-aperti-entrate-lancia-i-primi-servizi-open-data/); [lavoripubblici](https://www.lavoripubblici.it/news/stampa/35094). The WFS GetCapabilities at `https://wfs.cartografia.agenziaentrate.gov.it/inspire/wfs/owfs01.php` answered with feature types `CP:CadastralParcel` and `CP:CadastralZoning` (verified). My GetFeature attempt by bbox returned nothing usable, so attribute content (nationalCadastralReference = comune+foglio+particella) is unconfirmed
- **Geoportale Cartografico Catastale (verified to exist).** `https://geoportale.cartografia.agenziaentrate.gov.it/` redirects to `/age-inspire`. It is the interactive search and display of parcels, with no registration — [search summary of AdE communiqué 24/9/2020](https://agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/2665953/063_Com.+st.+Servizi+consultazione+cartografica+24.09.2020/72ef6a6a-4f9a-ab8c-1ecc-e1667e0d3858). Since 2020 the maps may be reused, commercially too, citing the source — [ediltecnico](https://ediltecnico.it/?p=11231). The free view does not include every map content — [biblus.acca](https://biblus.acca.it/mappe-catastali-al-via-il-servizio-di-navigazione-dinamica/)
- **Visura for one's own property: "Consultazione personale" (verified in AdE guide, Nov 2025).** It is free and limited to properties the requester holds real rights on. It gives the current visura (with intestatari) or the historical one, the map extract, the **planimetria** of the unità immobiliare urbana and the ispezione ipotecaria, as PDFs. Access is through the Fisconline/Entratel area with SPID, CIE or CNS — [AdE, "La visura catastale", novembre 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- **Visura for property not owned: online "Visura catastale" (verified, same guide).** It is free online since 1 Jan 2025 (D.Lgs. 139/2024 art. 7, Provvedimento 30/12/2024), through the same SPID/CIE/CNS reserved area. It gives current visure per soggetto, current or historical visure per immobile by dati catastali, and the map extract of a catasto-terreni particella. At a counter (sportello) the fee is €3 for a property the requester doesn't hold — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- **Planimetria is reserved (verified).** All cadastral information is public, "a eccezione della consultazione delle planimetrie riservata esclusivamente agli aventi diritto sull'immobile o ai loro delegati" — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf). So a professional gets a client's planimetria only as a delegato
- **SISTER (professionals):** the AdE platform for professionals' visure, ispezioni and planimetrie under a convention. I fetched no primary page with its 2025-26 tariffs or the delega mechanism. A CNI circular on SISTER exists (July 2024) but I did not read it — [CNI circ. 194/2024](https://ingegneri.vr.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/CIRC-CNI-194-Prot-CNI-7878U-11.07.24-SISTER.pdf)

#### 6. Impresa esecutrice
- **DURC On Line (secondary):** a regularity check can be requested on **any** impresa by codice fiscale by any authorised subject. The service is for registered users (SPID/CIE/CNS) on the INPS or INAIL portals. Any authorised user can also consult an already-issued DURC. INPS has published cooperation (application) specs, but these are for enabled bodies — [INPS spec, servizio cooperazione DurcOnLine](https://www.inps.it/content/dam/inps-site/pdf/allegatinews/2987KEY-d_allegato_1_specifiche_tecniche_servizio_cooperazione_durconline_inps.pdf); [fiscozen](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/verificare-durc-online/); [edilizia.com](https://www.edilizia.com/certificazioni/documento-unico-regolarita-contributiva/durc-on-line/). `www.durc.inps.it` did not respond (dead host). The service lives inside inps.it/inail.it
- **Visura camerale (secondary):** registroimprese.it (verified 200). An ordinary visura costs about €3 for a ditta individuale, €3.50 for società di persone and €5 for società di capitali, with storiche costing more (June 2026 guide). It's free for the company's own officers through the cassetto digitale at impresa.italia.it (SPID/CNS) — [fiscozen costi](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/costi-visura-camerale/); [studiocataldi](https://www.studiocataldi.it/articoli/38402-visura-camerale.asp). Sources disagree on whether buying one needs a registroimprese.it account

### Inferences
- Of all these services, only the AdE WMS/WFS and the Comune's static files and CKAN can be called from code without a person's login. The WMS gives geometry and a picture of the parcel number, not its identifiers as data. Getting foglio/particella from coordinates likely needs the WFS (attributes unconfirmed) or the INSPIRE bulk GML.
- The PRG zone of a particella can in principle be got offline by georeferencing the Tav. 6 PDFs, but the Comune says the online copies are illustrative. The legally valid answer is the CDU, or the architect reading the official plates, plus the 2026 variants (centro storico housing, attrezzature).
- Vincoli in Rete and the online visure (SPID) are behind personal logins. Automating them would mean driving a logged-in person's session, which falls under the project's "browser pane, person logs in" pattern rather than an API.
- SITAP's map shows art. 136/142 perimeters to anyone, but with no live WMS, only a person, or a scraper of an undocumented page, can read it. Its own and VIR's disclaimers say the data are not certified.
- The planimetria and the building-title history are not public data: the first goes to the owner or delegato, the second needs an accesso agli atti with a qualifying interest (the owner, or the professional on their behalf).
- Terms against automated access: none of the pages I read states a ban. AdE's WMS says "Dato pubblico" and the open data are CC-BY. VIR asks for attribution and calls its data uncertified. I did not read the terms of use for SISTER, Fisconline, INPS or registroimprese, and these normally forbid automated access to authenticated areas (unverified).

## Which facts are fully lookup-able by address or dati catastali, and which only the owner's documents or the architect's judgment can supply?

### Takeaway
By address alone you can get the municipalità/quartiere (stradario, offline) and approximate parcel geometry (AdE WMS). With foglio/particella you can also get the PRG zone (from the plates, illustrative), the plate-level vincoli (Tav. 12-14, SITAP) and the current visura (online, free but SPID). The legally binding zoning (CDU), the subalterno's planimetria, the chain of building titles and condoni (stato legittimo), cultural-heritage decrees on the specific building, and the firm's DURC each need the owner, a delega, a request to an office or a login. Whether the work fits CILA/SCIA, the tolerances and the conformity of the current state stay the architect's judgment.

### Cited Findings
- Public, anonymous, machine-readable: cadastral map WMS "Dato pubblico" — [AdE WMS](https://wms.cartografia.agenziaentrate.gov.it/inspire/wms/ows01.php?SERVICE=WMS&REQUEST=GetCapabilities); parcel open data CC-BY — [ediltecnico](https://ediltecnico.it/catasto-e-dati-territoriali-aperti-entrate-lancia-i-primi-servizi-open-data/); stradario CSV — [dati.comune.napoli.it](https://dati.comune.napoli.it/)
- Public but documents only (PDF/JPG, not GIS): PRG zonizzazione Tav. 6, centro storico Tav. 7, vincoli Tav. 12-14, NTA — [Cartografia](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/urbana-urbanistica-napoli/il-prg/cartografia/), [NTA](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/urbana-urbanistica-napoli/il-prg/norme-di-attuazione/); marked illustrative — [Variante generale](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/urbana-urbanistica-napoli/il-prg/variante-generale-al-prg/)

- Login (SPID/CIE/CNS), free: visura per immobile/soggetto, own planimetria — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf); DURC check — [fiscozen](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/verificare-durc-online/)

- Paid: visura camerale €3-5 — [fiscozen](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/costi-visura-camerale/)
- Owner or delegato only: planimetria — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)

### Inferences
- **By address (anonymous):** municipalità, quartiere, ASL distretto (stradario, already in `internal/napoli`). Approximate parcel location (WMS image; the identifier is read off the label).
- **By dati catastali (anonymous, interpretive):** PRG zona/sottozona and centro storico classification from plates 6/7, geomorphological, landscape and archaeological plate vincoli (12/13/14), seismic microzone (11), ope-legis/decreed landscape areas (SITAP, viewed by a person). Each is an illustrative reading, to be confirmed by a CDU or the architect.
- **By dati catastali with the person's SPID:** intestatari, categoria, classe, rendita, consistenza, superficie (visura). The planimetria only when the person is an avente diritto (or the professional is delegated).
- **Only from the owner's documents or the archive:** the titles that make up the stato legittimo (licenze, concessioni, DIA/SCIA/CILA, condono provisions, agibilità), the atto di provenienza, condominium authorisations, earlier asseverazioni. The archive is reachable only by accesso agli atti, an asynchronous request with no online status I found.
- **Only from the impresa:** DURC (anyone with SPID can verify it by CF), visura camerale (paid), plus what no registry gives (insurance, iscrizione cassa edile as such, the firm's staff).
- **Architect's judgment:** the intervention category (manutenzione straordinaria vs ristrutturazione, hence CILA vs SCIA), conformity of the current state with the titles, tolerances (art. 34-bis), whether a vincolo actually binds the specific works (e.g. a Parte II decree on the building, PTP zone rules), structural/seismic deposit needs.

### Gaps
- No single source answers "which vincoli bind this particella" with legal value except the CDU and Soprintendenza confirmation. No machine endpoint gives Napoli PRG zoning by coordinates.
- Whether the Comune's internal SIT exposes any public WMS (nothing found).
