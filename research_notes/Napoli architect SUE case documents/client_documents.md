# Client-side documents an owner or tenant in Napoli gives the architect for a CILA/SCIA at the SUE: field inventory, sources, examples

Scope: the documents a client brings for a residential CILA/SCIA (ID and codice fiscale, title deed or succession, nota di trascrizione, visura and planimetria catastale, lease or comodato plus the owner's assent, procura speciale, condominium papers, bills). Research done 2026-10-31. No personal data from real documents is reproduced. The AdE specimens quoted below use placeholder holders ("COGNOME Nome").

## 1. Which fields each document carries, in what format, and how reliable or current it is

### Takeaway
The **visura catastale** is the richest machine-readable source. It gives Comune (codice Belfiore), sezione urbana, foglio/particella/subalterno, zona censuaria, microzona, categoria, classe, consistenza, superficie catastale (total and without open areas), rendita, address with piano and scala, and each intestatario with CF, diritto and quota. It also shows the act each item comes from, and even the planimetria's filing date. The **planimetria** gives the drawn layout, scale and filing protocol, but proves nothing about urban-planning legitimacy except as primo impianto evidence for pre-titolo buildings. The **deed, nota di trascrizione and succession** prove title. The **lease, assent and procura** prove who may act.

### Cited Findings

**Visura catastale: official structure (AdE guide "La visura catastale", November 2025, with worked specimens)**
- A visura is issued by the Ufficio Provinciale - Territorio and contains: identifying and classamento data; the personal data of the cadastral intestatari; the address (urban units only); and the update and annotation causes. The planimetria and mappa are separate "visure". — [AdE guide Nov 2025 (mirror)](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- Header ("INTESTAZIONE DELLA VISURA"): AdE logo, issuing Direzione/Ufficio provinciale, date and time, visura number, type of catasto, type of visura, and the date the data refer to ("Situazione degli atti informatizzati al …"). — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- Sections: **DATI IDENTIFICATIVI** (identifiers with sezione if present, partita if present, and for fabbricati the corresponding catasto terreni parcels). **INDIRIZZO** (denominazione urbana, odonimo, n. civico, piano, scala; fabbricati only). **DATI DI CLASSAMENTO** (rendita, zona censuaria, categoria or qualità, classe, consistenza; for groups D/E only the categoria). **DATI DI SUPERFICIE** (total, and total excluding open areas, group A only; computed under DPR 138/1998). **INTESTAZIONE DELL'IMMOBILE** (name, surname, place and date of birth, codice fiscale; or denominazione for entities; the diritto reale, quota, regime patrimoniale and the act the intestazione comes from). Also DATI CONTABILI and a LEGENDA. — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- Every section is laid out in two columns. The left column holds the census data. The right column holds the "causale di aggiornamento e annotazione": the act that created or changed the identifier, a toponymy change, a classamento change, a surface change, or the transfers of the intestazione (deeds, successions, judgments). — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- The classamento right column carries the notification status of the rendita: "in corso", "in corso con prot. n… del…", "effettuata con prot. n… del…". — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- Specimen (fictitious, Ravenna), **visura attuale per immobile, catasto fabbricati**, read from the rendered page:
  - identifiers: "Comune di RAVENNA (H199) (RA) Foglio 24 Particella 4000 Subalterno 10, Partita: 2000000" and "Particelle corrispondenti al catasto terreni … Foglio 24 Particella 4000";
  - address line: street, n., "Scala A Piano 6";
  - classamento: "Rendita: Euro 666,23, Zona censuaria 2, Categoria A/2, Classe 1, Consistenza 6 vani";
  - superficie: "Totale: 349 m², Totale escluse aree scoperte: 349 m²", with the right column reading "Dati relativi alla planimetria: data di presentazione 11/02/2020, prot. n. …", i.e. **the visura states the filing date and protocol of the planimetria in atti**;
  - intestazione: "1. COGNOME NOME (CF …) Nato a ROMA … Diritto di: Proprieta' per 1/2 in regime di comunione dei beni con …", the right column citing the deed (date, notary's name and seat, repertorio, compravendita, nota presented with Modello Unico, n. …).

  — [AdE guide Nov 2025, §4.2.1 p. 14](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- Specimen (fictitious, Avellino), **visura attuale sintetica per soggetto**. It is a table with columns N., Sez. Urbana, Foglio, Particella, Sub, Zona Cens., Micro Zona, Categoria, Classe, Consistenza, Superficie Catastale, Rendita, Indirizzo, Dati derivanti da, Dati ulteriori. Example row: Foglio 16, Particella 120, Sub 2, Zona 3, A/2, classe 3, 4,5 vani, "Totale: 112 m² / Totale escluse aree scoperte: 105 m²", "Euro 537,48 / L. 1.040.700". Then an "Intestazione degli immobili" table with DATI ANAGRAFICI, CODICE FISCALE and DIRITTI E ONERI REALI ("Proprieta' per 1/2 in regime di comunione dei beni con …"), and a "DATI DERIVANTI DA" line naming the deed or succession (e.g. "DENUNZIA (NEI PASSAGGI PER CAUSA DI MORTE) … Voltura n. …"). A C/6 garage row shows consistenza in m². — [AdE guide Nov 2025, §4.1.2 p. 13](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- The surface excluding open areas is defined in the specimen's footnote: it leaves out "balconi, terrazzi e aree scoperte pertinenziali e accessorie" (Provvedimento del Direttore dell'Agenzia 29 marzo 2013). — [AdE guide Nov 2025, p. 13](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- Visura per soggetto: summary block (search scope, the subject's personal data, total count of terreni and fabbricati), then details per immobile (up to 10). Immobili with the same ownership are grouped under "Intestazione attuale dell'immobile/degli immobili n… – totale intestati: …". — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- Visura storica: lists, period by period, earlier identifiers, classamenti and intestazioni. Via personal consultation the storica comes **without** intestatari. — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- Meaning of the identifiers: the foglio is a portion of the comune's territory, the particella the building, the subalterno the single unit (fabbricati only). The sezione urbana precedes the foglio where present. The rendita for groups A/B/C = consistenza × tariffa for Comune, zona censuaria, categoria and classe. — [money.it](https://www.money.it/visura-catastale-cos-e-come-leggerla-cosa-contiene); [areasosta.com FAQ](https://areasosta.com/faq/qual-e-la-sezione-catastale)

**Planimetria catastale**
- Contents: the unit's perimeter and internal partitions, doors, windows, accesses, room use where given, piano and room heights, north orientation, adjoining parts (coerenze), exclusive pertinenze (balconies, terraces, gardens, cellars) if in the same scheda, a graphic scale and the scheda's identifiers. — [edilizia.com guide](https://www.edilizia.com/guide/planimetria-catastale-cose-a-cosa-serve-come-ottenerla/)
- Scale: 1:200 is the most common; 1:100, 1:50 and 1:500 are also allowed. — [edilizia.com guide](https://www.edilizia.com/guide/planimetria-catastale-cose-a-cosa-serve-come-ottenerla/)
- Top left: the header of the provincial Catasto office with the protocol number and date. — [edilizia.com (search snippet)](https://www.edilizia.com/catasto/planimetria-catastale-costo-dove-richiederla-e-a-chi-richiederla/)
- The visura itself reports the planimetria's "data di presentazione" and protocol, as in the AdE specimen above. — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- Primo impianto planimetrie were drawn to enrol urban units for the first time under R.D.L. 652/1939, usually at 1:200, with north orientation and the floor(s). — [studiotecnicopagliai.it](https://www.studiotecnicopagliai.it/planimetria-catastale-dimpianto-per-la-conformita-urbanistica-immobile/)
- **Stato legittimo.** For buildings built when no building permit was required, art. 9-bis c. 1-bis DPR 380/2001 lets the stato legittimo be read from the "informazioni catastali di primo impianto" or other probative documents, together with the titolo for the last intervention. — [studiotecnicopagliai.it](https://www.studiotecnicopagliai.it/planimetria-catastale-stato-legittimo-quando-sostituire-titolo-edilizio/); [ANCE Quaderno LP 02 Stato legittimo](https://ance.it/wp-content/uploads/allegati/Quaderno-LP-02-Stato-legittimo.pdf); [lavoripubblici.it guide to art. 9-bis](https://www.lavoripubblici.it/news/stampa/37313)
- Otherwise the planimetria does **not** prove legitimacy: "Il Catasto ha finalità principalmente fiscali e inventariali". A later planimetria matching today's state does not show that the works were authorised. — [edilizia.com guide](https://www.edilizia.com/guide/planimetria-catastale-cose-a-cosa-serve-come-ottenerla/); [ediltecnico.it](https://ediltecnico.it/87922/planimetria-catastale-e-legittimita-urbanistica/)
- A 1939 planimetria that differs from today's state is not by itself proof of an abuse (early drawings could be imprecise). Changes made after primo impianto without a titolo are still abuses. — [studiotecnicopagliai.it](https://www.studiotecnicopagliai.it/scheda-catastale-1939-diversa-stato-attuale-abuso-edilizio/)
- A 2026 TAR ruling (Sicilia 1427/2026): a CILA on a building without proof of stato legittimo leaves the works unlawful. — [lavoripubblici.it](https://www.lavoripubblici.it/news/cila-stato-legittimo-immobile-senza-titolo-tar-sicilia-1427-2026-38292)

**Atto di compravendita (rogito)**
- Since DL 78/2010 (art. 29 c. 1-bis L. 52/1985) a transfer deed must contain: the catasto identification; a reference to the planimetrie on file; and the intestatari's declaration that the cadastral data and planimetrie match the actual state. The notary checks that the intestatari match the catasto. — [Normattiva, L. 52/1985 consolidated](https://www.normattiva.it/eli/stato/LEGGE/1985/02/27/52/CONSOLIDATED); [diritto.it](https://www.diritto.it/conformita-urbanistica-e-catastale-degli-immobili/)
- Under art. 46 DPR 380/2001 and art. 40 L. 47/1985 the deed carries the "menzioni urbanistiche": the parties declare the titolo under which the building was built (or that it predates 1 September 1967), on pain of nullity. — [diritto.it](https://www.diritto.it/conformita-urbanistica-e-catastale-degli-immobili/); [lavoripubblici.it on the Notariato's July 2025 note](https://www.lavoripubblici.it/news/amp/stato-legittimo-compravendite-precisazioni-notariato-35962)
- **For the architect:** the deed's urban-planning mentions name the building titoli (licenza/concessione/permesso, condoni), the starting point for the stato legittimo search.

**Nota di trascrizione / ispezione ipotecaria**
- Layout of a nota: Quadro A (the act: type, date, notary or authority, repertorio), Quadro B (the immobili with cadastral identifiers and some descriptive data), Quadro C (the subjects "a favore" and "contro", with the diritti reali and quote on the Quadro B immobili), Quadro D (free-text extra information). — [AdE circ. 128/1995 (mirror)](https://www.studiopetrillo.com/files/circ_128_1995.pdf); [AdE Lombardia note 2023 on Quadro D](https://www.commercialisti.brescia.it/images/AGEDRLOM_156638_2023_981.pdf)
- The trascrizione is the formality that makes transfers public. Older acts (before mechanisation) need an "ispezione cartacea". — [areasosta.com FAQ](https://areasosta.com/faq/come-scaricare-nota-di-trascrizione-conservatoria)

**Contratto di locazione (canone concordato, Napoli)**
- The DM 16 January 2017 (MIT; GU n. 62 of 15/03/2017) sets the criteria for local agreements and the standard contracts: agevolato under art. 2 c. 3 L. 431/98, transitorio, and students. — [Gazzetta Ufficiale](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2017/03/15/17A01858/sg)
- Contracts modelled on that decree carry the unit's Foglio, Particella, Subalterno, Categoria catastale and Rendita catastale, plus pertinenze (box, cantina, posto auto). — [forms-legal.com template](https://forms-legal.com/it/italy/real-estate/leases/contratto-di-locazione-canone-concordato-3-2); [Regione Valle d'Aosta model](https://gestionewww.regione.vda.it/allegato.aspx?pk=46902)
- Napoli accordo territoriale, per a commercial secondary source:
  - signed by owners' associations APE, APPC, ASPPI, CONFABITARE, CONFAPPI, FEDERPROPRIETÀ, UPPI, UNIONCASA and tenants' associations SUNIA, ASSOCASA, UNIAT, UNIONE INQUILINI, SICET;
  - 7 areas, bands from 19 €/m² (area 1) to 150 €/m² (area 7);
  - contract "certification" by a signatory association needed for the tax benefits;
  - agevolato "3/4/5/6 anni + 2", transitorio up to 18 months with a documented reason, students 6–36 months;
  - the PDF path suggests an upload in 2025/07.

  — [dokicasa.it, Napoli page](https://dokicasa.it/contratti/locazione-canone-concordato-napoli). The sources disagree on dates: ANCE reports a Napoli agreement renewed on 7/11/2017. — [ANCE 2018](https://ance.it/2018/05/il-risparmio-energetico-premia-gli-affitti-concordati/)

**CIE (carta d'identità elettronica) and codice fiscale**
- CIE front: document number top right (format AA00000AA on cards issued since 29/09/2022), issuing comune, surname, name, place and date of birth, sex, height, citizenship, expiry, photo, and "non valida per l'espatrio" where it applies. — [lentepubblica.it](https://lentepubblica.it/pa-digitale/numero-carta-di-identita-elettronica-dove-trovarlo-e-caratteristiche/); [finanzadigitale.com](https://www.finanzadigitale.com/guide/numero-carta-identita-elettronica/)
- CIE back: codice fiscale in clear text and as a barcode, the MRZ below it, residence address, birth-record details, and parents' data only for minors. — [lexplain.it](https://www.lexplain.it/carta-didentita-elettronica-cie-ecco-cosa-sapere/); [geopop.it on the MRZ](https://www.geopop.it/carta-identita-elettronica-espatrio-come-funziona-sistema-mrz/)
- The secondary sources disagree on which fields sit on the front or the back. I found no official Ministry layout page in this session.

**Procura speciale (impresainungiorno)**
- Generation: the portal builds the procura text automatically, with the **pratica number** already in it, once the personal data are entered and the presenter ticks "IL PRESENTATORE DELLA DOMANDA È UN PROFESSIONISTA / ALTRO SOGGETTO MUNITO DI PROCURA SPECIALE". The procurator's data go in the box "QUALIFICA E DATI DEL PROCURATORE", never in the titolare's scheda anagrafica. — [Comune di Verona guide to impresainungiorno](https://www.comune.verona.it/content/download/8276/173275/file/Guida_alla_presentazione_di_pratiche_tramite_impresainungiorno.pdf)
- Signing: the client signs the downloaded form by hand ("firma olografa"). A PDF scan of a valid ID of the signer must be attached. The procura is valid for that pratica only (not a generic procura). The procurator keeps the originals and shows them on request. Otherwise the pratica is declared irricevibile. — [Comune di Verona guide](https://www.comune.verona.it/content/download/8276/173275/file/Guida_alla_presentazione_di_pratiche_tramite_impresainungiorno.pdf)
- The SUAP technical rules list "Procura speciale - dati e dichiarazioni (solo in caso di delega)" as a block of the pratica's summary model. — [impresainungiorno, SUAP allegato operativo](https://www.impresainungiorno.gov.it/documents/d/l-impresa-e-il-comune/suap-allegatooperativo); [coll.impresainungiorno, structure of a SUAP pratica](https://coll.impresainungiorno.gov.it/documents/155997/157492/Struttura+pratica+SUAP+e+integrazione+della+SCIA+in+ComUnica.pdf/247a5bb1-f664-491a-8a28-7189eda425fc)
- In a filed pratica's receipt the procura appears as a separate attachment, often a digitally signed .pdf.p7m. — [Regione Abruzzo BURA, SUAP receipt example](https://bura.regione.abruzzo.it/sites/bura.regione.abruzzo.it/files/bollettini/2025-10-06/3-ricevuta-suap-2.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Most reliable and current source for dati catastali, intestatari and quote:** the visura attuale per immobile. It is dated to "situazione degli atti informatizzati al …" and is free and instant online. Deeds and the nota record the situation at signing; a later succession, division or DOCFA variation can change both identifiers (a sub can be suppressed and renumbered) and owners. The architect should therefore re-pull the visura at filing time instead of copying identifiers from an old deed.
- The visura's right-hand column on the intestazione cites the provenance act (notary, repertorio, date) or the succession and voltura. A client who lacks the deed can be traced to it from the visura, then to the nota via an ispezione ipotecaria.
- The planimetria's filing date (shown on the visura) tells at once whether the scheda is a primo impianto (around 1939–1940s enrolment) or a later DOCFA variation. Only the former can feed art. 9-bis c. 1-bis, and only for buildings that needed no titolo when built.

### Gaps
- No public specimen of a **planimetria catastale** image from agenziaentrate.gov.it was retrieved. Field descriptions come from professional guides. The signature or redattore field on post-DOCFA planimetrie was not confirmed.
- No **specimen nota di trascrizione** PDF was retrieved. The quadro structure comes from circ. 128/1995 and an AdE regional note.
- No official **notariato.it** page on the structure of a compravendita deed was found (comparizione, premesse, oggetto, prezzo and payment method, provenienza, menzioni urbanistiche, conformità catastale, APE, impianti). Only the art. 29 and art. 46 elements above are sourced.
- I did not retrieve the **official text of the Napoli accordo territoriale** (date, current bands). Only a commercial page and an older ANCE note were found, and they disagree on dates.
- I did not retrieve the text of **Allegato A of DM 16/01/2017** (the standard contract: the clause on works by the tenant, APE and millesimi fields). The GU page served only the header.
- Not researched for lack of budget: **comodato** contents and registration, the **dichiarazione di successione** form (quadri EA/EB with dati catastali), **TARI Napoli bills** and **utility bills** (whether they show dati catastali or a POD/PDR usable for address checks). The verbale/regolamento condominiale contents were also not researched.

## 2. Where the client gets each document, time, cost, and whether the architect can get it directly

### Takeaway
Since 1 January 2025, visure catastali (attuale and storica per immobile, attuale per soggetto) are **free online for anyone** with SPID/CIE/CNS, owner or not. Planimetrie stay restricted to the holders of real rights or their delegates. A professional gets them through SISTER with a digitally signed declaration of the assignment. At a counter, a non-owner pays €3 per visura.

### Cited Findings
- Since 1/1/2025 online visure catastali are issued free of tributi even for immobili the requester does not own (D.Lgs. 139/2024 art. 7; Provvedimento 30/12/2024). At the counter, for immobili the requester does not own, the tributo is €3. — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- "VISURA CATASTALE" service (area riservata Fisconline/Entratel, via SPID, CIE, CNS or Entratel/Fisconline credentials): free even for non-owners. It gives visure attuali per soggetto (no storica per soggetto), attuali or storiche per immobile by identifiers, and the mappa. It covers all of Italy except Trento and Bolzano and delivers PDF. — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- "CONSULTAZIONE PERSONALE" service: free, only for immobili the requester holds rights on. It gives:
  - the visura attuale (with intestatari) or storica (without);
  - the mappa;
  - **the planimetria of the unit**;
  - the **ispezione ipotecaria** (list of formalities on the selected immobile, the single notes and, where available, the titolo).

  — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- Catasto data are public, "a eccezione della consultazione delle planimetrie riservata esclusivamente agli aventi diritto sull'immobile o ai loro delegati". — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- At the counter: any Ufficio provinciale - Territorio, or a sportello catastale decentrato at a comune. Owners get it free for their own immobili; otherwise the ipotecari and catastali tributi table applies. Historical paper records (registri di partita, old maps, Modelli 55) are consulted at the competent office. — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)
- Professional via **SISTER**: needs the specific enablement and a digitally signed request containing the declaration of the assignment from a holder of real rights. The planimetria is refused if the named subject is not an intestatario, or if their CF is not validated in Anagrafe Tributaria. The exception is an assignment from an **heir**, which can be filed without the intestatario's CF. — [informazionefiscale.it](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/planimetrie-catastali-consultazione-online-collaboratori-professionisti); [ediltecnico.it](https://ediltecnico.it/consultazione-planimetrie-catastali/)
- The delega must be kept in original for 5 years. Sources say it is valid 30 days from signing (stated for the estate-agent delega route). — [edotto.com](https://edotto.com/articolo/planimetrie-catastali-delega-online-allagente-immobiliare); [pmi.it](https://www.pmi.it/?p=183640)
- A tenant, neighbour or prospective buyer cannot obtain the planimetria just because of an interest in the unit. Other non-exempt requests (certificati, copie, estratti) cost €30 each under the D.Lgs. 139/2024 table from 1/1/2025. — [edilizia.com guide](https://www.edilizia.com/guide/planimetria-catastale-cose-a-cosa-serve-come-ottenerla/)
- Access to the Comune's building archive (titoli, for stato legittimo): the Comune cannot deny an owner access to the documents establishing the immobile's legal history (TAR Campania 1681/2022, a Napoli-region ruling). — [ediltecnico.it](https://ediltecnico.it/superbonus-e-documentazione-storia-immobile-il-comune-non-puo-negare-laccesso-agli-atti/)
- Citizens can now file the voltura catastale for a succession themselves. — [AdE guide Nov 2025](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf)

### Inferences
- **The architect can pull the visura per immobile directly (free, instantly)** from foglio/particella/sub, or a visura per soggetto from the client's CF. So the client really only has to supply the CF and the address. The planimetria needs either the owner's own SPID download (Consultazione personale, free) or a SISTER request backed by the owner's delega.
- **Tenants:** a tenant cannot get the planimetria themselves. The owner must download it or delegate.
- **Owners:** the owner can download the ispezione ipotecaria (notes and, where digitised, the titolo itself) free through Consultazione personale. That can replace asking the notary for a copy of the deed when the titolo is in the digitised registers.

### Gaps
- No official AdE page giving turnaround times was retrieved. Online visure are immediate per the guide's description of PDF delivery; SISTER planimetria lead times are unconfirmed.

## 4. Which facts from these documents the CILA/SCIA form needs

### Takeaway
The unified CILA model asks the titolare's identity (from the CIE), the **titolo** ("in quanto … proprietario, comproprietario, usufruttuario, amministratore di condominio…"), whether the title is **exclusive** (otherwise the holders of other rights must give their assent), whether **parti comuni** are touched, and the location: street, n., scala, piano, interno, CAP, catasto fabbricati/terreni, foglio, map., sub., sez., sez. urb., destinazione d'uso. The procurator's data come from the procura. Almost all of this is on the visura (identifiers, address, piano and scala, intestatari and quote) plus the CIE and the lease.

### Cited Findings
- Fields of "DATI DEL TITOLARE": Cognome e Nome, codice fiscale, nato a / prov. / stato, nato il, residente in / prov. / Stato, indirizzo, n., C.A.P., PEC / posta elettronica, Telefono. Repeatable in the "SOGGETTI COINVOLTI" annex for several titolari. — [Regione Lazio CILA model](https://www.regione.lazio.it/sites/default/files/2026-07/Allegato%204%20-%20Comunicazione%20inizio%20lavori%20asseverata.pdf)
- "DATI DEL PROCURATORE/DELEGATO (compilare in caso di conferimento di procura)": Cognome, Nome, codice fiscale, birth, residence, PEC, phone. — [Regione Lazio CILA model](https://www.regione.lazio.it/sites/default/files/2026-07/Allegato%204%20-%20Comunicazione%20inizio%20lavori%20asseverata.pdf)
- "a) Titolarità dell'intervento": "di avere titolo alla presentazione di questa pratica edilizia in quanto ____ (Ad es. proprietario, comproprietario, usufruttuario, amministratore di condominio etc…)". Then either a.1 "avere titolarità esclusiva all'esecuzione dell'intervento", or a.2 not exclusive "ma di disporre comunque della dichiarazione di assenso dei terzi titolari di altri diritti reali o obbligatori". — [Regione Lazio CILA model](https://www.regione.lazio.it/sites/default/files/2026-07/Allegato%204%20-%20Comunicazione%20inizio%20lavori%20asseverata.pdf)
- "b) Opere su parti comuni o modifiche esterne", one of:
  - b.1: no parti comuni;
  - b.2: parti comuni of a condominium; the amministratore must hold the assembly's approving delibera;
  - b.3: common parts of a multi-owner building not constituted as a condominium, approved by all co-owners in "soggetti coinvolti" with their IDs;
  - b.4: common parts but no assent needed under art. 1102 c.c.

  — [Regione Lazio CILA model](https://www.regione.lazio.it/sites/default/files/2026-07/Allegato%204%20-%20Comunicazione%20inizio%20lavori%20asseverata.pdf)
- "e) Localizzazione dell'intervento": "sito in (via, piazza, ecc.) ___ n. ___ scala ___ piano ___ interno ___ C.A.P. ___ censito al catasto fabbricati / terreni foglio n. ___ map. ___ (se presenti) sub. ___ sez. ___ sez. urb. ___ avente destinazione d'uso ___ (Ad es. residenziale…)". — [Regione Lazio CILA model](https://www.regione.lazio.it/sites/default/files/2026-07/Allegato%204%20-%20Comunicazione%20inizio%20lavori%20asseverata.pdf)
- Attachments table:
  - "Procura/delega", when the comunicazione is filed by procura or delega;
  - "Soggetti coinvolti", always;
  - "Copia del documento di identità del/i titolare/i e/o del tecnico", only if they did not sign digitally and/or there is no procura;
  - "Dichiarazione di assenso dei terzi titolari di altri diritti reali o obbligatori", if the title is not exclusive.

  — [Regione Lazio CILA model](https://www.regione.lazio.it/sites/default/files/2026-07/Allegato%204%20-%20Comunicazione%20inizio%20lavori%20asseverata.pdf)

### Inferences
Fact → which client document it can be read from:
- titolare name, CF, birth, residence → CIE (front and back), or the CF card. Birth and CF are also on the visura's intestazione for owners. Residence is not on the visura.
- titolo → for an owner, the visura intestazione "Diritto di: Proprieta' per 1/1" or "per 1/2" (→ proprietario vs comproprietario), "Usufrutto", "Nuda proprietà", backed by the deed, nota or succession. For a tenant, the registered lease (locatario). For a comodatario, the registered comodato.
- titolarità esclusiva → exclusive only if the visura shows a single intestatario at 1/1 and no usufrutto. With co-owners, an usufructuary, or a tenant or comodatario as applicant, the a.2 route needs the **assenso** of the other intestatari listed on the visura (name and CF read off it).
- localizzazione: via, n., scala, piano → the visura's Indirizzo section (odonimo, civico, "Scala A Piano 6"). Interno and CAP are usually not on the visura: they come from the deed, the lease or a bill.
- foglio, particella, sub, sez. urb. → the visura's Dati identificativi. In Napoli the sezione urbana is relevant: the Comune is divided into sezioni urbane, which precede the foglio. This is from background knowledge, not verified in this session.
- destinazione d'uso → the visura categoria (A/2–A/4 → residenziale). The legal destination is the one in the titolo edilizio, not the catasto.
- procurator's data → the procura speciale (impresainungiorno-generated, with the pratica number, signed by hand, with the signer's ID).
- parti comuni → the regolamento or verbale condominiale (assembly delibera) for b.2. All co-owners' signatures and IDs for b.3.
- stato legittimo → the deed's menzioni urbanistiche (titoli cited), plus the Comune's archive. The primo impianto planimetria only for buildings that needed no titolo when built.
