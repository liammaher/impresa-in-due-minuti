# Read the visura first, ask the architect last

A typical Napoli apartment renovation (new bathroom, moved tramezzi, new impianti, no structural work) is a **CILA under art. 6-bis DPR 380**. It costs **€151 in diritti**, paid before filing, and since **6 July 2026** it can be filed only on impresainungiorno, with every file signed digitally. It becomes a **SCIA (€401)** once structural parts are touched, which also brings in the Genio Civile seismic filing. Any façade or window work under a vincolo needs an **autorizzazione paesaggistica** first, as a separate procedure. Most of what the pratica asks sits in four places. The **visura catastale** gives identifiers, address, intestatari, quote, categoria, surface and the planimetria's filing date; anyone with SPID can pull it free since 2025. The **deed** gives the titoli edilizi in its menzioni urbanistiche. The **Comune's building archive**, reachable only by accesso agli atti, holds the stato legittimo. The **architect's design choices** settle the rest. Only a thin layer can be looked up anonymously: the stradario, the cadastral map, the PRG plates (which the Comune calls illustrative) and the SITAP landscape map. The fastest pipeline for Claude reads the dump in this order: visura, deed, identity documents, the architect's drafts. Each fact goes into the vault without passing through the conversation. Then the router and pre-flight get pre-filled proposals. By my estimate this cuts the **~63 questions** a classic CILA still asks after routing to about **15–20 real questions**, plus a batch of "ok" confirmations. Calendar time is a different matter. It depends on the archive search and the prior assents, which no software shortens. The biggest risk, a filing declared **irricevibile**, comes from checks pre-flight doesn't run yet: PDF/A, file names, a digital signature on every file, the procura, and the postal-account receipt.

## A €151 CILA becomes a €401 SCIA and a prior landscape procedure

The classic case is set in Napoli's own CILA notes. Manutenzione straordinaria under art. 3 c.1 lett. b includes "l'apertura di porte interne o lo spostamento di pareti interne, sempre che non riguardino le parti strutturali". To classify the works, the architect reads art. 3 and art. 6-bis DPR 380, **art. 10 of the NTA of the Variante generale al PRG** and Tabella A of d.lgs. 222/2016, and the state definitions prevail over local ones ([CILA note illustrative 23/06/2025](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)). Under **DD 501/2026** and its Linee Guida, PdC, SCIA, CILA, CIL, agibilità and autorizzazione paesaggistica all go "esclusivamente" through impresainungiorno. PMM, PEC and paper are refused as irricevibili ([DD 501/2026 and Linee Guida](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/LINEE-GUIDA_30.06.2026.pdf)). The platform went live on **Monday 6 July 2026** ([Comune di Napoli, 02/07/2026](https://www.comune.napoli.it/novita/piattaforma-impresa-in-un-giorno/)). One note in this research said Napoli still used its own servizi online and had no PDF/A or size rule. Those were 2025 pages, and DD 501/2026 supersedes them.

Napoli adds several requirements of its own, and each one is a way to fail. The fee table updated 16/06/2026 sets **CILA €151**, **SCIA and gratuitous PdC €401**, **autorizzazione paesaggistica €401** (€441 in sanatoria) and the CILA tardiva (MCILA) at €166 in diritti ([Tabella diritti](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/tabella-diritti-agg.16_06_2026.pdf)). The older €128 figure on secondary sites is out of date. The diritti go **only to conto corrente postale 57060964**, "Comune di Napoli Servizio Tesoreria Edilizia Privata", with the causale "N1051_SUE – Tipo Pratica, Identificativo, Anno". Any other payment makes the pratica "IRRICEVIBILE" ([Edilizia Privata – Costi](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/urbanistica-patrimonio-politiche-per-la-casa-cimiteri-cittadini/edilizia-privata/costi/)). The forms Napoli adopted always require the diritti receipt, the regional **Modello DP** (the professional declares the client paid their fees, L.R. 59/2018) and **Allegato A "Nuovo Programma 100"**, the applicant's sworn declaration on their position with Comune di Napoli taxes ([DD 603/2025](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/0603_2025.pdf); [Programma 100](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Allegato_A_nuovo_p100.pdf); [Modello DP](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/MOD.DP_Dichiarazione_di_Pagamento.pdf)). Since 2026 a **Stato Comparativo** sheet is mandatory: **yellow for demolitions, red for new construction**, landscape layout, dimensions and a north arrow. Files must be **native PDF/A, at most 20 MB and 200 DPI**, with names free of spaces and accents. **Every document is signed digitally.** A filing is irricevibile when a form or procura lacks a digital signature, when someone other than the applicant files without a procura, or when a file isn't PDF/A or is over 20 MB ([Linee Guida DD 501/2026, §§3–9](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/LINEE-GUIDA_30.06.2026.pdf)). In **zona A** every CILA or SCIA also carries a sworn attestation (art. 481 c.p.) that the building matches its coded **tipo edilizio** (art. 63 c.4 of the Variante) ([CILA note](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)).

The variant, case (b), forks in two directions. Touching structure makes it a SCIA under art. 22 and adds a filing on Regione Campania's S.I.smi.C.A. portal at the **Genio Civile di Napoli**, under L.R. 9/1983 and Reg. 4/2010. Modello A covers autorizzazione, deposito or attestazione; Modello B covers works "privi di rilevanza" ([DD Campania 362/2020 models](https://benevento.ordingegneri.it/wp-content/uploads/sites/74/2022/03/D_D_%20N_%20362%20DEL%2005_08_2020%20CON%20ALLEGATI(1).pdf); [Reg. 4/2010](https://benevento.ordingegneri.it/wp-content/uploads/sites/74/2022/03/REGOLAM_4_2010_coordinato.pdf)). Touching the outside of a building under a vincolo brings in DPR 31/2017. Interior works that don't alter the outside are exempt under Allegato A.1, but new or changed openings and façade work fall under the Allegato B simplified procedure. In Napoli that autorizzazione "va acquisita come procedimento autonomo e presupposto prima dell'inoltro della Cila" ([servizionline Edilizia Privata](https://servizionline.comune.napoli.it/en/web/napoli/edilizia-privata); [DPR 31/2017](https://www.bosettiegatti.eu/INFO/NORME/STATALI/2017_0031.htm)). The Comune's Area Ambiente runs it: the Commissione locale per il paesaggio gives an opinion, then the Soprintendenza ABAP gives the binding one ([MIT conference file, SABAP-NA opinion](https://mit.gov.it/nfsmitgov/files/media/conferenza-servizio/2025-01/Prot_17248_17092024_Parere_SABAP-NA.pdf)). UNESCO status alone triggers nothing. Only a specific art. 136 declaration on the area, or an art. 10/13 listing of the building, does ([ANCE](https://ance.it/2014/04/centri-storici-il-punto-sulla-tutela/?print=pdf)).

The slow part of all this is not the form. It is the **stato legittimo**. Napoli makes the architect list "gli estremi dei titoli abilitativi rilasciati riferiti al fabbricato" ([Linee Guida §7](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/LINEE-GUIDA_30.06.2026.pdf)). A June 2026 TAR ruling held that a CILA on a building whose stato legittimo can't be shown is ineffective ([lavoripubblici.it, TAR Sicilia 1427/2026](https://www.lavoripubblici.it/news/cila-stato-legittimo-immobile-senza-titolo-tar-sicilia-1427-2026-38292)). The titles sit in the SUE's archive, reachable by accesso agli atti through the SUE PEC (sportello.unico.edilizia@pec.comune.napoli.it). The law gives the Comune 30 days to answer, but Napoli publishes no form, fee or typical wait ([Servizio SUE](https://www.comune.napoli.it/amministrazione/unita_organizzativa/servizio-sportello-unico-edilizia/); [edilizia.com on access timelines](https://www.edilizia.com/edilizia-urbanistica/accesso-atti-edilizi-richiesta-tempi-costi-silenzio-comune/)). CILAs are then checked by random sample and can be declared inefficaci long after the works ([CILA note](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/CILA_note_illustrative_23_06_2025-1.pdf)).

The pattern is lopsided. The **visura alone answers the location block, the titolo, titolarità esclusiva and the list of people whose assenso is needed**. The unified model's localizzazione line ("via … n. … scala … piano … interno … C.A.P. … foglio … map. … sub. … sez. urb. … destinazione d'uso") maps almost field for field onto the visura's sections. Interno and CAP are the exceptions: they come from the deed, the lease or a bill ([Lazio CILA model](https://www.regione.lazio.it/sites/default/files/2026-07/Allegato%204%20-%20Comunicazione%20inizio%20lavori%20asseverata.pdf)). The visura also cites the provenance act in its right-hand column, so a client without the deed can be traced to the notary and repertorio, and from there to the nota through an ispezione ipotecaria. It even dates the planimetria. A filing date around 1939–40 means a primo impianto that can support the g.2 epoch route. A later date means a DOCFA variation, which proves nothing about legitimacy ([AdE guide](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf); [ediltecnico](https://ediltecnico.it/87922/planimetria-catastale-e-legittimita-urbanistica/)). Two cautions apply. A visura copied from an old deed can be stale, because successions and DOCFA variations renumber subalterni. So the identifiers should be re-pulled at filing time. And the visura's categoria proposes the destinazione d'uso without deciding it: the legal use is the one in the titolo edilizio.

## What a machine can look up, what only papers hold, what only judgment decides

Four sources answer without any login, and none of them is legally binding. The **AdE cadastral WMS** ("Dato pubblico") returns parcel geometry, but a GetFeatureInfo test in central Napoli gave back only a bounding box, with no foglio or particella as data. The number appears only as a label drawn on the image. The newer **WFS** (CC-BY) lists `CP:CadastralParcel`, but a bbox query returned nothing usable, so its attributes are unconfirmed ([AdE WMS](https://wms.cartografia.agenziaentrate.gov.it/inspire/wms/ows01.php?SERVICE=WMS&REQUEST=GetCapabilities); [ediltecnico on AdE open data](https://ediltecnico.it/catasto-e-dati-territoriali-aperti-entrate-lancia-i-primi-servizi-open-data/)). The **PRG** comes as static PDFs: Tav. 6 zoning at 1:4000, Tav. 7 centro storico at 1:2000, Tav. 11 seismic microzoning, Tav. 12–14 geomorphological, landscape and archaeological vincoli. The Comune calls the online copies "illustrative", and two 2026 variants (centro storico housing, DCC 129/2026; attrezzature, DGC 330/2026) sit on top of the 2004 plates ([Cartografia](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/urbana-urbanistica-napoli/il-prg/cartografia/); [Variante generale](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/urbana-urbanistica-napoli/il-prg/variante-generale-al-prg/); [SUE contatti](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/urbanistica-patrimonio-politiche-per-la-casa-cimiteri-cittadini/edilizia-privata/contatti/)). The **stradario** is open data, and the vault's `internal/napoli` already turns an address into municipalità, quartiere and ASL distretto offline ([dati.comune.napoli.it](https://dati.comune.napoli.it/)). **SITAP** shows art. 136 and 142 perimeters to anyone, but its WMS did not respond, so only a person in a browser can read it ([SITAP](http://sitap.cultura.gov.it/)). The Comune has no public webGIS, and the PMM portal with its online CDU did not answer.

Behind a login or a request sit the facts about the specific unit. The visura per immobile is free with SPID for anyone. The **planimetria is reserved** "agli aventi diritto sull'immobile o ai loro delegati": the owner downloads it, or a professional with a signed delega uses SISTER ([AdE guide](https://www.dirittobancario.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Guida-Agenzia-delle-Entrate-novembre-2025-la-visura-catastale.pdf); [informazionefiscale](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/planimetrie-catastali-consultazione-online-collaboratori-professionisti)). Vincoli in Rete, for Parte II listings of buildings, needs an account and calls its own data uncertified ([VIR login](http://vincoliinrete.beniculturali.it/VincoliInRete/vir/utente/login)). The binding zoning (the CDU, from cdu@comune.napoli.it), the building titles (SUE PEC) and the condono files (antiabusivismo PEC) all need a request to an office ([Servizio Pianificazione](https://www.comune.napoli.it/amministrazione/unita_organizzativa/servizio-pianificazione-urbanistica-generale-e-attuativa/); [Condono](https://www.comune.napoli.it/articolo_tematico/urbanistica-patrimonio-politiche-per-la-casa-cimiteri-cittadini/condono-edilizio/)). A DURC can be checked by anyone with SPID from the firm's codice fiscale. A visura camerale costs about €3–5 ([fiscozen DURC](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/verificare-durc-online/); [fiscozen visura costs](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/costi-visura-camerale/)).

## The fastest path from a document dump to a filed draft

Some facts of the user's system shape this path; they are stated here as given. The runner fills impresainungiorno SUE drafts from one steps file per pratica. The **CILA steps file has 20 sections, 287 portal fields and 82 asks**: 16 text, 17 yes/no, 12 choice, 4 date, 9 file, 23 checklist items and 1 number. The **SCIA has 34 sections, 630 fields and 253 asks (48 of them files)**. The **permesso di costruire has 34 sections, 560 fields and 241 asks**. route.py first asks the rules table's facts (works type under art. 3, structures, prospetti, cambio d'uso, landscape regime, sanatoria) and picks the steps file. `run.py preflight` then asks only what the case needs, in one message, and takes **proposals**: answers drawn from known data that the person confirms with "ok". After routing, a classic CILA still has **about 63 questions**, many of them conditional. People and property come from the vault: identity, residenza, albo and business per person, and the property as `immobile.*` (indirizzo through catasto_subalterno, categoria, superficie_mq, destinazione_uso, titolo), with municipalità, quartiere and ASL computed offline. Values are piped from the vault, so personal data never passes through the conversation. Pre-flight checks that each file exists, that it is **under 9.77 MB per portal slot**, and that a .p7m is readable with the signer's codice fiscale. It does **not** check PDF/A or file names.

That last constraint shapes the first design choice: **the extraction must keep personal data out of the conversation too**. A visura is a text PDF in a fixed AdE layout: header, DATI IDENTIFICATIVI, INDIRIZZO, CLASSAMENTO, SUPERFICIE, INTESTAZIONE, each with a right-hand column. A local parser can therefore write `immobile.*` and the intestatari straight into the vault. Claude then sees only which fields were filled and the non-personal technical facts, such as categoria A/2, a planimetria filed in 1940, or "two intestatari at 1/2". A parser is my inference from the specimen layout; I have not built or tested one. The same applies to the CIE back (CF and residence) and to the deed's menzioni urbanistiche, which are mostly titolo numbers and dates rather than personal data.

The steps below are ranked by the architect's time they save. The "order" column gives when each step runs. Step 5 is the exception: it ranks lower but has to start first, because it is the only one with a calendar wait.

Put together, the about 63 post-routing asks divide roughly as follows. About **40–45 become proposals**: the 9 files, most of the 23 checklist items, titolo and esclusività, the stato legittimo route and titoli, the impresa, "no notifica", the residential use, the Napoli seismic zone once confirmed, and the tolerance surface band. The band should be proposed from the drawn floor area, with the visura's DPR 138/1998 superficie catastale only as a sanity check, because the two are different measures. About **15–20 stay real questions**, all of them the architect's professional judgment or plans: the art. 3 category and Tabella A activity if no relazione is in the dump, the free-text description of the works, the four dates, parti comuni (b.1–b.4) without a condominium delibera, the DM 37/08 impianti letters, the L. 13/89 status, energy and acoustics status, the tolerances actually declared, the conformity statement, and the uomini-giorno estimate. These counts are my estimate from the ask types; I have not counted them against the steps file. The "ok" message should list proposals grouped by source ("from the visura of 31/10/2026", "from the deed, notary X, rep. Y"), so the architect confirms a source rather than 40 separate values. The pre-flight message should then ask the remaining questions in dependency order. That puts parti comuni before the condominium attachment, and the impianti letters before the DM 37 project slot.

Six gaps lie outside what the system covers today. First, **PDF/A, the 200 DPI limit and file names** are Napoli irricevibilità causes that pre-flight doesn't check. Pre-flight's **9.77 MB slot limit is stricter than Napoli's 20 MB**, so a legal 15 MB drawing set still has to be split or downsampled to fit a slot. Second, **signatures**. Napoli requires every file signed digitally and refuses unsigned procure. Pre-flight reads only .p7m files, not PAdES-signed PDFs. The vault's sign gate stamps a handwritten-signature image, which the vault itself says is "not a firma digitale". The impresainungiorno procura is generated inside the draft with its pratica number, so it can't be in the original dump: the run has to pause, hand it to the client, and attach it signed. The Verona guide describes a hand-signed procura plus an ID scan ([Verona guide](https://www.comune.verona.it/content/download/8276/173275/file/Guida_alla_presentazione_di_pratiche_tramite_impresainungiorno.pdf)). Napoli's Linee Guida list procure "prive di firma digitale" among the irricevibili. Which signature Napoli accepts on the client's side is unconfirmed. Third, **the stato legittimo search** has no online status and no published Napoli procedure. The system can prepare the facts but not speed the answer, and open condoni and mismatched licenza plans stay outside it. Fourth, **prior procedures**. No steps file exists here for the autorizzazione paesaggistica, though Napoli files it on the same platform under DD 501. The Genio Civile filing sits on a different regional portal. Fifth, **channel conflicts**. DD 913/2024 and DD 603/2025 sent the MCILA and the sanatorie to paper at Palazzo San Giacomo, while DD 501/2026 says platform only. Where the COM inizio and fine lavori go now is also unconfirmed ([DD 913/2024](https://static-www.comune.napoli.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/disposizione_dirigenziale_913_2024_approvazione_circolare_adempimenti_sanatoaria_edilizia.pdf)). Sixth, **payment**. The postal account is the only lawful channel on the Costi page, and whether impresainungiorno takes PagoPA for Napoli is unknown. Two smaller facts are also unverified: Napoli's seismic zone (believed to be zone 2) and whether Napoli's catasto uses sezioni urbane, which the vault's `catasto_sezione` field would carry.

## Conclusion

The bottleneck is not the 287 portal fields but the gap between documents and judgment. The visura, a free and fixed-format AdE PDF since 2025, is the best single entry point for an assistant. It answers the location, the titolo and the people whose assent is needed, and it points to both the deed and the primo impianto that the stato legittimo depends on. Building the visura and file-classification steps first turns most of a CILA into confirmations. The architect's remaining questions are then exactly the ones the law makes theirs to answer under art. 481 c.p.

The case for automation is therefore protection against irricevibilità more than speed. Napoli's 2026 rules (PDF/A, ASCII names, every file signed, a procura only the draft can generate, one postal account) turn small formatting slips into a rejected filing. A system that checks these rules mechanically saves more than one that fills fields fast. The next gains lie outside the runner: a paesaggistica steps file for case (b), and an archive-request step that starts the stato legittimo clock the day the dump arrives.
