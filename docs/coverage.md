# Napoli portal coverage

Where the map of impresainungiorno stands, counted from the catalogue and the rehearsals by
`claude/skills/pratica/portal/scoreboard.py --write` (run it with the pratica venv's Python, or `make coverage`).
`docs/ready.md`, written by the same command from the same counts, lists what the released plugin ships.
Three claims, each stricter than the last:

- **Mapped**: the pratica is crawled (`catalogue/F839/sections/`): we know its sections and boxes.
- **Fillable**: mapped, and the dictionary fills every section of it, so `gen.py` can write its
  steps file. Nothing has touched the portal yet.
- **Run on the portal**: fillable, and a rehearsal generated from it has a run in its
  `runs.json` (the README's run table) that **filled** the draft: every value read back on a fresh load, with made-up
  people. Never declared, paid, signed or sent: that is the person's, by design.

"Drift-checked" counts the pratiche whose sections a drift check has compared with the live
portal (a changed one is refused by the runner); an unchecked pratica may have changed since its crawl.
A count here is a floor on what works, not a promise about a real case: a real case can take a
branch no rehearsal took.

## SUE completeness

**100% for the SUE means all of these**, for the 27 operations Napoli's SUE offers (and the combinations of two it allows):

1. Every operation is crawled, and every section of it is in the dictionary.
2. Every box of every screen is filled at every answer a real case can give: no refusal left in a recipe except one the law itself makes (a case that needs a human, said as such).
3. The attachments screen is read for every answer that changes it, and every slot is mapped or named as the person's (a signed `.p7m`, a drawing).
4. A drift check of the live portal against the crawls finds nothing new, and is repeated.
5. Each pratica has been filled on the portal from a blank draft, and a real case has been checked by someone who files that pratica.

### What each gap needs

- **2 cells to run**: run it in the pane with made-up people (40-150 s; needs Liam logged in).
- **0 cells to map**: add the missing sections to the dictionary (draft.py drafts them; a person checks).
- **0 cells to crawl**: crawl it in the pane on the scratch draft (20-60 s each; needs Liam logged in).
