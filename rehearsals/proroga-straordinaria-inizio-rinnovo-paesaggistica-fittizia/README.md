# Proroga straordinaria inizio e rinnovo paesaggistica, one pratica

Why a case files them together (`catalogue/F839/sue-pairs.yaml`, `authorization-keeps-pace`, reading): The landscape authorization lasts five years from the building title's efficacy, plus one to finish works begun (art. 146 c. 4 D.Lgs. 42/2004); a new title or an extension of a title's terms doesn't extend it. When the works' new term passes the authorization's, the filing that sets it carries what keeps the authorization in force: its art. 10-septies extension, before it expires; its renewal (art. 7 DPR 31/2017), expired no more than a year, the works unchanged and not or only partly done; or a new authorization, past that.
