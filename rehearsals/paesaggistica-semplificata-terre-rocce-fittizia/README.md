# Paesaggistica semplificata e terre e rocce, one pratica

`paesaggistica-semplificata` + `dichiarazione-utilizzo` selected together in the wizard on Napoli's SUE. Why a case files them together (`catalogue/F839/sue-pairs.yaml`, `dichiarazione-no-title`, reading): Works that need no building title in a protected area take the landscape procedure alone (artt. 146, 149 D.Lgs. 42/2004); if they dig and the soil leaves the site, the dichiarazione is due before the excavation too (art. 21 c. 1 DPR 120/2017): one pratica carries both.

- **The crawl**: `catalogue/F839/sections/sue-combo-paesaggistica-semplificata-dichiarazione-utilizzo.json`.
- **The recipe** `extends` `paesaggistica-semplificata-fittizia`, `scavo-fuori-sito-fittizio`: the first is the main pratica, the others' answers fill what its don't say.
