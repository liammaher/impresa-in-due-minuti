The ordinaria rehearsal's job (rehearsals/paesaggistica-ordinaria-fittizia: a wooden pergola and new
flooring on a roof terrace under landscape protection) found done in 2022 without the
authorization. Same architect with procura and client, made-up data; the draft is "PROVA NON
INVIARE" and stops before the declarations, signing and sending.

`portal-steps.generated.yaml` is `gen.py`'s, from `recipe.yaml`, the crawl of an empty draft
(`catalogue/F839/sections/sue-accertamento-di-compatibilita-paesaggistica.json`) and
`dictionary/fnsuap.yaml`. Twelve of its sixteen sections are the ordinaria's; its own four are the
works and what they changed, the technician, art. 181 (the dichiarante's presa visione) and the
date of the works.

## What it covers (2026-10-30)

The SUE rules table (`rules/sue-napoli.yaml`) gives this pratica (`napoli/comune/accertamento-
compatibilita-paesaggistica`) for works that need no building title (edilizia libera) but needed
the landscape authorization, semplificata or ordinaria, already done, with no new usable surface or
volume. Art. 167 c. 4-5 and art. 181 c. 1-ter, 1-quater D.Lgs. 42/2004 checked on Normattiva.

Refused at pre-flight, with the reason:

- Works that created usable surfaces or volumes, or increased lawful ones: not ascertainable (art.
  167 c. 4 a), the restoration of the place remains.
- Works that also need a building title: the building sanatoria with the accertamento isn't mapped.
- As the ordinaria: art. 134 alone, a tenant, the bollo by marche.

Left to the person: the relazione paesaggistica, the drawings of the state as it is and the photos
(signed), the scheda professionisti e imprese, the declarations (art. 181's presa visione among
them), signing and sending, and later the sum the Comune sets (the greater of damage and profit).
