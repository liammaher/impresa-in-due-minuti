Works done without the permesso (art. 36 DPR 380/2001) in a protected area, with no new usable
surface or volume: the building sanatoria holds only with the landscape compatibility found (art.
146 c. 4, art. 167 c. 4-5 D.Lgs. 42/2004). The portal lets the two be selected together in one
pratica (crawled 2026-11-02, `catalogue/F839/sections/sue-combo-pdc-sanatoria-accertamento.json`:
every section is one of the two operations', the dictionary fills them all).

The made-up case: the street façade of a building under art. 136 and 142, changed in October 2020
(two windows widened to French windows, new plaster and colour), the CILA rehearsal's people.

It can't be filed as the law asks: the permesso's asseverazione, quadro 16, wants one of three
procedures once the works alter the place (semplificata, ordinaria, the accertamento of art.
36-bis) and none is the art. 167 one (`defects.yaml` `pdc-36-paesaggio`, `docs/portal-defects.md`).
The recipe refuses the case with that defect; the steps stay mapped for the day the quadro has
the line.
